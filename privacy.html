Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Editarmorai (Harshit Jain, video editor and motion graphics artist) handles information on www.editarmorai.com and in the "Editarmorai Claude Connector" app that connects to Pinterest.

1. Website

The website does not ask you to create an account. If you contact us by email, phone, WhatsApp or Instagram, we receive the details you choose to send (such as your name, email address, phone number and message) and use them only to reply and to discuss your project.

2. Pinterest connector

The Editarmorai Claude Connector is a private tool used only by the account owner. It connects the owner's own Pinterest account to an AI assistant through the Pinterest API so the owner can search Pins, view their own Boards and Pins, and see trends for creative reference research.

  • It only accesses the Pinterest account that authorised it, with the permissions granted on Pinterest.
  • It does not access other Pinterest users' private data.
  • Data from Pinterest is used only to answer the owner's own requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to build user profiles.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only if required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

Messages are kept only as long as needed for the conversation or project. You can ask us to delete any information you sent us by writing to the email below. Pinterest access can be removed at any time by revoking the app in your Pinterest account settings.
